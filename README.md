## 💡 About the Project
Urban lakes and water bodies face severe degradation due to floating plastic debris, organic waste, and surface pollutants. This project models an **Autonomous Surface Vessel (ASV)** designed to tackle water pollution sustainably. 

The simulation visualizes a solar-powered catamaran hull equipped with computer vision sensors, an automated debris collection conveyor belt, and GPS/IMU navigation modules designed to map and clean polluted aquatic environments without human intervention.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Mechanical Design

* **Catamaran Hull Architecture:** Dual-hull configuration ensuring high stability, low draft for shallow waters, and optimal payload capacity for collected waste.
* **Solar-Powered Propulsion:** Rooftop photovoltaic solar panels providing continuous clean energy trickle-charging for onboard battery storage and electric thrusters.
* **Automated Debris Collection:** Modeled front-end conveyor belt mechanism designed to scoop up floating plastics and organic debris into an internal storage bay.
* **Computer Vision & Sensors:** Simulated vision systems for real-time floating waste detection and classification.
* **Autonomous Navigation:** GPS and IMU waypoint mapping for pre-programmed grid cleaning patterns and obstacle avoidance.
* **Geo-Tagged Waste Mapping:** Real-time data logging capabilities to record pollution hotspots and cleanup progress.
